What is a game jam?

A 12-hour sprint where teenagers gather to design, code, draw, and compose video games from scratch! 100% beginner friendly—no prior experience needed.

Learn and Build

Learn to code, draw pixel art, compose 8-bit sound effects, and use engines like Godot, Kaboom.js, Unity, Pygame & Scratch with step-by-step workshops!

Godot Engine (2D & 3D)

Free & lightweight! Perfect for 2D pixel platformers and 3D games.

Make Friends

Over half of participants arrive solo! Join our fun speed-friending icebreakers to form a 2-4 person dream team with fellow teen creators in Chennai.

The Coding Wizard: You bring game mechanics to life! You connect player controls, write game physics, and fix bugs.

Free Food and Prizes

Enjoy 100% free hot pizza, samosas, boba tea, snacks & chai during the 12-hour event! Compete for trophies like Best Game, Best Pixel Art, Best Sound & Fan Favorite!

🍕 Fresh Hot Pizzas 🧋 Boba Tea & Chai 🏆 Trophies & Keyboards 🎒 Custom Swag Bags

How 12 Hours of Game Jam Magic Happens!

1

Secret Theme Reveal

Morning Check-in: We unveil the secret theme! Solo attendees meet up to form dream teams.

2

Brainstorm & Build

Sketch your game ideas, choose your engine, draw sprites, and code your game prototype.

3

Food & Music Sprints

Fuel up on hot pizza & samosas, compose 8-bit chiptunes, and get help from expert mentors.

4

Arcade Showcase

Evening Finale: Submit your game, playtest everyone's creations, and win trophies & swag!
